This page was generated from the `lesson` archetype. It's a step up in opinion from `workshop`: the body is pre-structured to mirror how strong workshop lessons read end-to-end — *what you'll learn* up top, *concept* in the middle, *walkthrough* for the hands-on portion, *wrap up* that pivots forward.

Use `lesson` when you want the structure decided for you. Use `workshop` when you want a blank canvas.

## Generated by

## Wrap up

One paragraph summarising what the reader just built or learned, and a pointer to the next lesson in the chapter. The `lesson` archetype's wrap-up is intentionally short — by the time the reader gets here they want forward momentum, not a summary they could write themselves.
